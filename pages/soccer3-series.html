More than a match.

Soccer3 is the weekly competitive environment. Soccer3 Series is what happens when those sessions are given a season arc — fixtures, standings, and a series conclusion. Every result matters. Every round builds toward something.

The standard still applies.

Competitive stakes do not change the DBX framework. ROOT DNA standards, formal observation, and developmental assessment apply in Soccer3 Series exactly as they do everywhere else in the system. The series is competitive — and it is still DBX.

The Competition Product

What Soccer3 Series Is.

Soccer3 Series transforms recurring competition into a structured season — with formal fixtures, cumulative standings, and a series champion crowned at the finale.

01

Structured Season

Soccer3 Series runs as a defined season — a set number of rounds played across the term, with each round building on the last. Players know the fixtures in advance and can track their standing throughout.

02

Live Standings

Every result is recorded and reflected in live standings. Position is earned round by round — not handed to anyone. The table shows exactly where every player stands at every point in the series.

03

Series Finale

Every Soccer3 Series concludes with a structured finale event. Top-ranked players from the regular season compete for the series title. It is the highest-stakes moment in the Soccer3 competition calendar.

The Series Format

Round by Round.

Each Soccer3 Series runs across four rounds plus a finale. Every round has formal fixtures, recorded results, and updated standings. Nothing carries over from the previous series — each season begins at zero.

Round 01–03

Group Stage

All registered players compete in structured fixtures across three rounds. Points are awarded for wins and draws. The standings table builds week by week.

Round 04

Qualification

The final group-stage round determines which players qualify for the series finale. Top performers from the standings table advance. Others are formally reviewed ahead of the next series.

Round 05

Finale

The Soccer3 Series Finale. Qualified players compete in a structured knockout or round-robin format to determine the series champion. The most important day on the Soccer3 calendar.
