Every DBX membership begins with an enquiry — not a payment. DBX recommends the right route for your player based on where they are, where they are headed, and what the system observes.

How It Works

The DBX Intake Journey.

01

Enquire

Submit your enquiry. DBX will review the information you provide and respond with a recommendation.

02

Consent

A completed DBX Player Registration & Consent Form is mandatory before any player participates.

03

Taster

Your player attends a complimentary taster. DBX observes across all relevant development dimensions.

04

Observe

Following the taster, DBX completes a formal observation and makes an informed recommendation.

05

Join

DBX issues a membership invitation alongside the Parent Guide. Membership begins on agreed terms.
