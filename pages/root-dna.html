Core Principles

The Three Laws of ROOT.

Everything inside DBX is measured against these three principles. They are non-negotiable.

01

Behaviour Before Progression.

DBX does not move players forward just because they are talented. Readiness must be visible in behaviour, attitude and standards — consistently, not occasionally.

02

Identity Before Performance.

The player becomes stronger when they understand who they are becoming, not just what they are trying to win. Character is built before trophies are earned.

03

Pressure Reveals Habits.

ROOT helps players recognise the habits they repeat under pressure, then rebuild those habits through the DBX environment. Pressure is not a problem — it is the lesson.
