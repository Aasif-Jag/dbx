Competition is the revealer.

Coaching cannot fully reveal a player. Development sessions show potential — but Soccer3 shows reality. The pressure of live competition exposes exactly which qualities are genuinely embedded and which are still being built.

More games. More decisions.

The small-sided format of Soccer3 is deliberate. Players touch the ball more. Make more decisions per minute. Face more pressure cycles in a single session than they would in a full-sided match. The data density is unmatched.

The Competition Format

Small-Sided. High-Density.

Soccer3 uses a structured small-sided competition format designed to maximise decision frequency, technical demand, and observable pressure moments per player.

The Format

3v3 Rolling Competition

Short, structured games played in rotation. Every player competes in multiple matches per session — maximising exposure to live decision moments and competitive pressure cycles.

S3

Player Load

High Ball Contact

Players interact with the ball significantly more often than in full-sided formats. More contacts means more decisions, more pressure, and more observable evidence per session.

Observation

Structured Observation

DBX coaches observe and record against a defined framework — not just watching. Technical execution, decision quality, mental response, and behaviour are all tracked formally during play.

The Four Reveal Areas

What Soccer3 Exposes.

Live competition cannot be rehearsed. These four dimensions become visible under match pressure in a way no training session can fully replicate.

Reveal 01

Technical Under Pressure

Can the player execute what they have practised when it actually matters? Technical ability trained in isolation must transfer to live competition — Soccer3 shows whether it has.

Reveal 02

Decision Quality

Every action in Soccer3 is preceded by a decision. The quality, speed, and consistency of those decisions across a full session form one of the most important pieces of developmental evidence DBX collects.

Reveal 03

Mental Speed Live

Perception, decision, and recovery are formally assessed in the training environment — but Soccer3 is where those scores are either confirmed or contradicted. Live competition is the true Mental Speed test.

Reveal 04

Behaviour in Competition

ROOT DNA standards apply in Soccer3 exactly as they do everywhere else in DBX. How a player conducts themselves when losing, when winning, and when challenged reveals the depth of their behavioural foundation.

How A Session Works

Four Phases. Every Session.

Every Soccer3 session follows the same structured sequence. Each phase serves a distinct purpose — together they form a complete competitive development environment.

Phase 01

Activate

Players prepare mentally and physically. Expectations for the session are set. Focus is directed toward the specific development areas being observed.

Phase 02

Compete

Structured 3v3 games played in rotation. Players face multiple opponents across the session — maximising the number of distinct pressure situations each player experiences.

Phase 03

Intervene

Targeted mid-session coaching interventions. Specific moments are paused, reviewed, and addressed — linking live competitive situations directly to the development framework.

Phase 04

Review

Session data and observations are consolidated. Players receive direct feedback referenced against their individual development targets. Evidence is formally recorded.

Training shows what a player can do.
Soccer3 shows who they are.

The gap between training performance and competitive performance is one of the most revealing metrics in player development. Soccer3 makes that gap visible — and then makes it smaller.
