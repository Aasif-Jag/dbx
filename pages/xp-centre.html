Structure is the method.

XP Centre runs in structured term blocks, not open-ended training. Every session has a purpose. Every player is held to the same standard. The environment is designed — not improvised.

Standards, not sessions.

Attendance alone does not produce development. Every player who enters XP Centre is assessed, measured, and reviewed. Progress is based on evidence — not how long they have been in the system.

The Development Method

Four Domains. One Session.

Every XP Centre session addresses all four development domains simultaneously. No domain is optional — all four are tracked.

Domain 01

Technical

Ball mastery, first touch, movement patterns, and execution under pressure. Technical ability is trained through repetition, precision, and progressive demand — not just practice.

Domain 02

Tactical

Decision-making, positioning, game intelligence, and reading situations before they develop. Tactical understanding is built through structured scenarios with real consequences inside every session.

Domain 03

Behavioural

Attitude, effort, standards, and ROOT DNA alignment. How a player behaves when challenged, corrected, or fatigued is as important as what they can do with the ball. This domain is never optional.

Domain 04

Mental

Speed of thought, response to pressure, recovery from mistakes, and self-regulation. The mental domain links directly to Mental Speed — measured formally across the DBX system.

How A Term Works

Four Phases. Every Term.

Each XP Centre term block follows the same four-phase structure. There are no shortcuts between phases — each must be completed before the next begins.

Phase 01

Assess

Every player begins the term with a structured assessment across all four development domains. This establishes a baseline and informs the work that follows.

Phase 02

Build

Structured development work targets the specific areas identified in the assessment. Technical, tactical, behavioural, and mental work runs simultaneously across the term.

Phase 03

Apply

Players are placed into competitive and game-realistic scenarios where development must be applied under genuine pressure. Application is where the real test begins.

Phase 04

Review

Formal assessment of the term. Performance data, behaviour records, and developmental evidence are reviewed against DBX standards. Progression decisions are made here.
