The Competitive Environment

What XP Games Is.

XP Games is a structured competitive showcase — more formal, more observed, and more demanding than Soccer3. It is where the highest-performing players in the DBX system are placed to be tested together.

01

Elevated Standard

Every player at XP Games has been observed, assessed, and selected. The standard of competition is higher because the standard for entry is higher. There is no easy opponent here.

02

Formal Observation

XP Games events are formally observed against the full DBX framework. Technical execution, decision-making, Mental Speed, and behavioural standards are all recorded — with the data feeding back into each player's development record.

03

Progression Evidence

Performance at XP Games forms part of the evidence base used in formal DBX reviews. It informs decisions about the Panther Pathway, XP Passport entries, and future development targeting.

The Selection Process

How Players Are Chosen.

Selection for XP Games is based on cumulative evidence across four criteria. All four must be met. No exceptions, no partial selections.

01

ROOT DNA Standing

The player's behavioural record across the DBX system must meet the ROOT DNA standard. Attitude, standards, and conduct are assessed. No player with unresolved ROOT concerns is selected for XP Games.

02

XP Centre Standard

Development across all four XP Centre domains must be tracking at the required standard. Assessment data from the player's most recent XP Centre term review informs this criteria directly.

03

Soccer3 Performance

Competitive performance across Soccer3 sessions is reviewed. Decision quality, technical application, and conduct under match pressure are all considered as part of the XP Games selection process.

04

Mental Speed Rating

The player's formal Mental Speed assessment must meet the required rating. Cognitive readiness for elevated competition is a specific threshold — players below the rating are not yet selected.
