The invisible game.

Before a player moves, they must perceive. Before they act, they must decide. Every physical outcome on the pitch is preceded by a cognitive event — and Mental Speed measures the speed and quality of those events.

Speed of thought, not speed of foot.

Physical pace can be trained. Cognitive speed is where elite players separate from the rest. DBX measures the mental dimension formally — because what cannot be seen cannot be developed without a system.

The Core Framework

Three Dimensions. One Speed.

Every Mental Speed assessment is built on three measurable cognitive dimensions — the complete cycle from input to recovery.

01

Perceive

The speed at which a player reads environmental information — space, pressure, teammates, opponents — before they receive the ball. The elite player perceives what most players miss entirely.

Perception Speed
02

Decide

The quality and speed of the action selection that follows perception. A good decision made slowly is often as costly as a bad decision. DBX measures both dimensions — speed and outcome quality — simultaneously.

Decision Rate
03

Recover

How fast a player resets mentally after an error, a setback, or a moment of pressure. Recovery speed determines whether one mistake becomes two — or whether it becomes nothing.

The Measurement System

Four Methods. One Picture.

Mental Speed cannot be measured by one test. DBX builds a complete cognitive profile through four structured measurement approaches applied across environments.

Method 01

Scenario Reaction

Players are placed in structured game scenarios and observed for perception speed — how quickly they identify the relevant cue and begin the correct action.

Method 02

Decision Quality Review

After competition and practice, specific decision moments are reviewed against a structured framework — measuring not just what was done, but why, and whether the information required was available.

Method 03

Pressure Response Tracking

Coaches track decision-making behaviour specifically in moments of elevated pressure — fatigue, losing positions, mistakes. Recovery speed and mental composure are formally recorded.

Method 04

Longitudinal Comparison

Mental Speed scores are tracked over time across XP Centre terms and Soccer3 sessions. Developmental trends reveal whether cognitive speed is improving, stalling, or regressing.

The Rating System

Five Levels. One Target.

Every player receives a Mental Speed rating after each formal assessment. The target is not a number — it is consistent, elite-level cognitive performance under pressure.

Level 01

Foundation

Player is beginning to process game information consciously. Perception and decisions are reactive rather than anticipatory.

Level 02

Developing

Awareness is improving under low-pressure conditions. Decisions are more consistent but still slow when pressure increases.

Level 03

Applied

The player can apply cognitive speed under moderate pressure. Decisions are timed better. Recovery from error is becoming reliable.

Level 04

Advanced

Consistent performance under high pressure. The player anticipates — they have already processed the situation before the ball arrives.

Level 05

Elite

Cognitive speed is a competitive weapon. Perception, decision, and recovery all operate at the level required to sustain elite performance.
